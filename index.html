📈 Progressive Overload Philosophy

🎯 How to Progress (in this exact order):

  1. Start with a weight that allows proper technique and the prescribed rep range.
  2. Increase reps gradually while keeping the same weight.
  3. Once you reach the top of the rep range in every set with good technique and 1–2 reps in reserve, increase the weight by the smallest increment.
  4. After increasing the weight, reps may drop toward the lower end of the range — that's normal.
  5. Record the weight and reps for every set.
  6. Don't increase the weight every week automatically.
⚠️ Important: The listed machine weights are starting references, not guaranteed targets. Machine resistance varies. Prioritize controlled technique, suitable range of motion, and consistent progress.

⏱️ Rest Periods

  • Compound exercises: 2–3 minutes
  • Isolation exercises: 60–120 seconds
  • If performance is dropping because you're not recovered, rest longer.

🔄 Deload Guide

What is a Deload Week?
A planned "easy week" every 4 weeks. Same exercises but reduced weight and sets. After it, you'll typically feel stronger — called Supercompensation.
  • Weight: -10% from current working weight
  • Sets: Cut 1 set from each exercise
  • Reps: Keep the same
  • Rest: Increase to 2–3 minutes
  • Duration: Max 45 minutes

Signs you need a Deload now:
  • Persistent fatigue, poor sleep
  • Joint aches, low motivation
  • Performance stalled or declining 2-3 sessions in a row
